## What the chain taught, in one place

The one guide-only segment that stayed sane for its first five seconds held the camera at 0.07 px against 0.21 and 0.34 on the still-anchored takes, and the next guide-only segment drifted 0.67 px, so there is no rule about guide-only camera hold, one sample each way. The nine frame guide did carry motion across the seam both times (mean difference 3.4 to 4.9 of 255 against 8.1 for ordinary motion eight frames apart), and both guide-only runs hallucinated in the same place; one seed is one sample, and two seeds in one shape is a pattern worth one more test, which is what he ruled. The still-anchored first frame sits 0.06 to 0.33 px from the frames after it, which is Tee's to weigh and is below anything a viewer sees. And the A5 card itself carries a conflict the chain exposed: its prompt says the grey-coat woman "stands still where she stopped", while the SH150 end still it must land on has her at mid distance, walking, and has the maroon-scarf woman gone. The stills win over the prompt in a locked-camera chain, so the generated middle follows the stills; whether the SH150 composite or the standing rule is canon for the end of the shot is a ruling, and it goes on a card. He ruled the still. The end-anchored v03 did not hallucinate where the two guide-only segments did, on one seed, so the hypothesis holds on one sample; and it bowed about as far in its middle as guide-only v02 (0.66 px against 0.67) before the anchor pulled it back, so on this evidence the end anchor fixes the ending and the composition and not the camera, and a 19 s take is the longest this lane has held under the gate at both ends.

## What the TSWS n8n estate offers the clips, read live

Read from the VPS instance between 08:38Z and 08:48Z with `get_workflow_details`, read only. A first sweep of all 25 TSWS workflows by 25 `scanner` agents returned 25 failed reads and 2.7 million tokens of repository evidence, because that agent type carries Read, Grep and Glob and no MCP tool at all; a connector read is a job for the main session or a general-purpose agent, and that is now written into CLAUDE.md under the workflow stages.

`TSWS 01 Post-Production Master` (55 nodes, active, last edited 2026-10-21) scans `/data/tsws/drop/*/manifest.json` on a schedule and runs an episode end to end: a manifest with `piece_id`, `narration` and `shots` (`[{path, in, out, generated}]`, seconds, `generated` true unless set false) is validated, every generated shot goes through `TSWS 04 Detail Recovery` unless `skip_recovery` is true (the master's own notes call that pass Topaz and warn that a restart burns its credits twice), the cut is assembled by `TSWS 03`, the audio mastered by `TSWS 02`, the mark rendered and composited, the master conformed by `TSWS 05`, and packaging written with the OS 28 gates, three of which read `needs_human`. Since 2026-10-21 it also routes a `lane: micro` manifest to two `TSWS Micro` workflows, which are not readable over MCP until their cards enable it. `TSWS 03 Visual Assembly` (19 nodes) builds one `assemble_cut` job on the render worker: every shot conformed to `width`, `height` and `fps` (defaults 3840x2160 at 24), one chained xfade of `crossfade` seconds between shots (default 1.0), letterbox in #050308, CRF 16, then a duration gate against `target_duration` with 0.5 s of tolerance that halts the episode and writes no `.done`. `TSWS 05 Conform and Grain` (25 nodes) is one encode pass, lanczos to 3840x2160, 10 bit, grain 4, x265 at CRF 16, with a PSNR gate against a native reference (floor 42, skipped when no reference is passed) and a fall through to the unconformed master on any failure. All three are the Cloud workflows imported on 2026-08-31 and repaired on 2026-09-15, each ending in a `Return To Caller` node that hands the result rather than the log receipt back.

What that means for the LTX clips: a clip enters the episode as one `shots[]` entry with `generated: true`, and three defaults would touch it on the way through. The detail recovery pass would run on it, the assembly would conform it from 1920x1080 to 3840x2160 and dissolve one second of it into each neighbour, and the conform would scale it again. The joins the cards are built on are a frame the two sides share, which a one second dissolve would smear, and `crossfade` is set once per manifest for every cut in the episode; so either the LTX clips are placed in the ChatCut cut as the cards say, which is the route ruled on 2026-10-31, or the manifest carries `crossfade: 0` and `skip_recovery: true` for an episode that runs through the master. That is a design question for Tee, not a defect in the workflows.

The LTX lane itself has no n8n workflow. `TSWS SH070 - 05 LTX 2.3 Masked Repair` (24 nodes, active, `callerPolicy` same owner, never run on real media by its own note) is the orchestration shape: a `/system_stats` read that refuses a CPU device, an `/object_info` read that checks every node and input of the built graph against the installed schema, `POST /prompt`, then `/history/{id}` polled every ten seconds with a 120 poll cap and a provenance check on the output manifest. It is pointed at the VPS ComfyUI (`comfy.2-24-109-87.sslip.io/_worker`, header auth credential `kBOP7WZ3k8oirkXW`, CPU only) and names models the pod does not hold (`ltx-2.3-22b-dev` with a distilled LoRA and an inpaint LoRA). Pointing it at a pod is a URL, a credential and the pod's node set, and the `/object_info` check is the part worth keeping. `TSWS EP01 Street Keyframes 2026-10-17 DRAFT Caller` (inactive) holds three never run Gemini image jobs for masked street stills through the Google Media Runner, and `TSWS EP01 Worker Health` (inactive) is one GET on `172.16.2.1:8080/health`.
