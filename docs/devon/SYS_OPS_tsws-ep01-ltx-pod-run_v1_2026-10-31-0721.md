## What the TSWS n8n estate offers the clips, read live

`TSWS 01 Post-Production Master` (55 nodes, active, last edited 2026-10-21) scans `/data/tsws/drop/*/manifest.json` on a schedule and runs an episode end to end: a manifest with `piece_id`, `narration` and `shots` (`[{path, in, out, generated}]`, seconds, `generated` true unless set false) is validated, every generated shot goes through `TSWS 04 Detail Recovery (EditForge)` unless `skip_recovery` is true, the cut is assembled by `TSWS 03`, the audio mastered by `TSWS 02`, the mark rendered and composited, the master conformed by `TSWS 05`, and packaging written by `Build Packaging`. The first version of this paragraph called the recovery pass Topaz and said a restart bills its credits twice, from three stale notes in the master; its headline note says in bold that detail recovery no longer uses Topaz and runs on the render worker as denoise, deflicker, upscale and deband behind a QC gate, with no API key and no credits, and a critic read both at 09:17Z. Packaging writes six gates, not the five its note names and not the three this paragraph first counted: brand, authorship, platform policy, monetization, disclosure and qc. Platform policy and monetization read `needs_human` always; brand, authorship and qc fall to it unless the item carries a rendered thumbnail, an `authored_by` or a `qc_verdict` of SHIP or WAIVED; disclosure reads true or false only, false whenever any shot is `generated` and `ai_disclosed` is not true. The item that reaches packaging descends from `Validate Manifest`, whose output carries none of `authored_by`, `qc_verdict` or `ai_disclosed` (those keys occur nowhere in the 55 nodes outside packaging itself), so through the master authorship and qc read `needs_human` on every run, disclosure reads false on every episode with a generated shot, and `publish_allowed` is false. That is the safe direction for a lane that never publishes without a human, and a gap all the same: the master cannot pass a disclosure it is never given. Since 2026-10-21 it also routes a `lane: micro` manifest to two `TSWS Micro` workflows, which are not readable over MCP until their cards enable it. `TSWS 03 Visual Assembly` (19 nodes) builds one `assemble_cut` job on the render worker: every shot conformed to `width`, `height` and `fps` (defaults 3840x2160 at 24; the master emits `target_w` and `target_h` while 03 reads `width` and `height`, so a manifest size never reaches the assembly through the master), one chained xfade of `crossfade` seconds between shots (default 1.0; the worker accepts 0 to 10), CRF 16, then a duration gate with 0.5 s of tolerance that halts the episode and writes no `.done`. The gate reads `target_duration` first, which the master never passes (the string occurs nowhere in its 55 nodes and the call to 03 maps no inputs), so through the master it falls back to the sum of the shots minus the crossfades and checks the worker against its own arithmetic rather than the cut against the narration; 03's note says to pass the narration length there. 03's notes also say the letterbox pad is #050308; no node passes a colour, and the worker's normalise chain in this repository (`deploy/render-worker/jobs.js`) pads black, so a shot that needs padding gets black, and whether the deployed worker matches this build is unverified. `TSWS 05 Conform and Grain` (25 nodes) is one encode pass, lanczos to 3840x2160 (a 1:1 scale by then, since the assembly conformed every shot already), 10 bit, grain 4, x265 at CRF 16, with a PSNR gate against a native reference (floor 42, skipped when no reference is passed) and a fall through to the unconformed master on any failure. All three were created on 2026-08-31. 03 and 05 were repaired on 2026-09-15 and each ends in a `Return To Caller` node that hands the result rather than the log receipt back, which is what made them callable from 01 at all; the master has no such node, is the scheduled top-level pipeline, ends in its done marker or a halt, and shows a last edit of 2026-10-21.

What that means for the LTX clips: a clip enters the episode as one `shots[]` entry with `generated: true`, and the defaults would touch it three ways on the way through. The detail recovery pass would run on it (the worker's denoise, deflicker, upscale and deband), the assembly would conform it from 1920x1080 to 3840x2160 and dissolve one second of it into each neighbour, and the conform would add grain and re-encode it to 10 bit x265 without scaling it again. The joins the cards are built on are a frame the two sides share, which a one second dissolve would smear, and `crossfade` is set once per manifest for every cut in the episode. The first version of this paragraph proposed `crossfade: 0` as the way through; a critic ran the worker's own xfade line and that drops most of the following clip, because an xfade of duration 0 ends the output at its offset: two 1 s clips at 24 fps came out as 26 frames of 48, reproduced here on ffmpeg 6.1.1 at 09:22Z (26 of 48 at duration 0, 47 of 48 at one frame), and 03's duration gate would then halt the episode. The smallest cut the nodes support is a one frame crossfade, `crossfade: 0.041667` at 24 fps, not zero. And `generated: true` alone makes disclosure read false and `publish_allowed` false for every episode carrying an LTX clip, since the fields packaging reads cannot reach it today. So either the LTX clips are placed in the ChatCut cut as the cards say, which is the route ruled on 2026-10-31, or the manifest carries `crossfade: 0.041667` and `skip_recovery: true` for an episode that runs through the master, with the disclosure field wired from the manifest to packaging first.
