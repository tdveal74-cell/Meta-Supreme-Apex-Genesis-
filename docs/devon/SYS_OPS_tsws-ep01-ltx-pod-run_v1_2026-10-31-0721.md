## What the chain taught, in one place

The guide-only segment holds the camera better than a still-anchored one (0.07 px against 0.21 and 0.34) and carries motion across the seam, and on its first seed it hallucinated. One seed is one sample; the lesson is written as a sample and not as a rule. The still-anchored first frame sits 0.06 to 0.33 px from the frames after it, which is Tee's to weigh and is below anything a viewer sees. And the A5 card itself carries a conflict the chain exposed: its prompt says the grey-coat woman "stands still where she stopped", while the SH150 end still it must land on has her at mid distance, walking, and has the maroon-scarf woman gone. The stills win over the prompt in a locked-camera chain, so the generated middle follows the stills; whether the SH150 composite or the standing rule is canon for the end of the shot is a ruling, and it goes on a card.
