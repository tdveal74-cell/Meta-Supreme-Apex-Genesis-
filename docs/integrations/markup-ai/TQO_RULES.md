# TQO proposed Markup AI rules, v0.1 (review only)

**Voice:** calm, precise, proof-driven, anti-hype, quietly confident. No fear-mongering, robotic filler, false urgency or unsupported assurance about AI career outcomes.

**Audience:** mid-career professionals, roughly 35–50, looking for concrete actions they can verify to prepare for workplace AI changes.

**Source and truth rules:** never invent first-person tests, employer experience, clients, conversations, studies, dates or measured results. Source figures must match approved research receipts. Flag claims requiring an independent check. An editorial score is not source verification.

**TQO script rules:**
- Specific opening in the first 10–15 seconds. Learning objective inside 30 seconds.
- Long-form narration typically 1,200–2,000 spoken words; 3–5 step checklist and a repeatable viewer action.
- End with one natural question and the word `comments` in the last spoken sentence. Do not append a like/subscribe/download/audit CTA.
- Cutaway B-roll must not replace the owned presenter with anonymous office workers.
- Synthetic likeness and voice disclosure are reviewed in the actual release package.

**Prohibited text for TQO script scope:** `actually`, `leverage`, `probably`, `maybe`, `really`, `free audit linked below`, `like and subscribe`. Also flag `in today's fast-paced world`, `revolutionary`, `crush it`, `experts say`, `studies show`, `reports suggest`. Treat `passive income` as a contextual review warning, not unconditionally false.

**Punctuation:** no em dash, en dash or semicolon in delivered copy. Restructure the sentence.

**Advisory writing patterns:** flag repetitive “It's not X, it's Y” constructions, generic promotions, needless repetition, and AI-sounding phrasing. Keep ordinary words `can`, `just`, `that`, `it`, `may`, `could` permitted.

**Important scope separation:** the public website's additional house-word rules are not automatically global script bans. TSWS has a separate locked 99/100 canon. NCO Forge, EditForge and WhatBroke require independent voice profiles.

**Release:** Script Doctor >=90/100 is required for TQO under the 2026-10-19 ruling, along with all evidence, rights, disclosure, policy and authenticated human approval gates. Markup AI must not assign or bypass the Script Doctor score.
