# ACX viral output

Drafts content shaped to the patterns that tend to earn views in consciousness,
cosmic and esoteric material, while protecting precision and refusing invented
claims. The line that matters most in this niche is the one between what a
viewer observed and what the channel believes it means. Every draft keeps that
line visible.

Whoever fronts ACX on screen is Tee, his wife, or a character the two of them
voice under recorded consent. Never a rented or stock persona.

DEVON's Area registry, `services/devon/areas.py`, still defines ACX as the
Ascension Caudex micro drama IP. This skill covers the direct address awakening

whether the registry line changes, is his ruling and has not been made.

## Niche

Viewers already in or entering consciousness, cosmic and esoteric inquiry. They
respond to direct address, personal signs, frequency language and clear next
steps. They reject generic self-help and motivational slogans.

## Working patterns

These are house hypotheses taken from watching the niche. They are not measured
on our channel. Treat them as defaults to test, and replace one the day a
channel number disagrees with it.

Patterns that tend to work:

- An identity hook in the first 3 seconds that selects the viewer
- One specific emotional, perceptual or synchronicity sign instead of abstract philosophy
- One consciousness concept mapped to one concrete personal experience
- A sense of timing without fear: something is underway, framed as the channel's reading
- On-screen text that names the sign or the concept
- A simple practice or observation the viewer can do today
- Staying with one concept for the whole piece

Patterns that tend to fail:

- Abstract theory with no personal anchor
- Stacked lists of signs with no detail
- Promises of guaranteed manifestation or any health result
- Invented light codes, frequencies or numerical protocols presented as fact
- Generic law of attraction slogans
- Recruiter style spiritual branding

## Voice rules

- Direct. One claim per sentence.
- Anchor every cosmic or consciousness term to an emotion, a perception or an observable event
- Short sentences next to one longer explanatory sentence
- Calibrated confidence: say what is observed and what remains interpretive, in those words where it helps
- No em dashes, no en dashes, no semicolons, straight quotes only
- No empty intensifiers
- Where this skill and `tee-voice` disagree, `tee-voice` wins

## Hard lines

- Physics words are metaphor here. "Quantum", "observer effect", "frequency" and "entanglement" are used as the channel's interpretive language and are never presented as what physics has shown. When a line borrows a physics term, the same breath marks it as a reading, not a finding.
- No health claims. A body sensation is never named as a sign of awakening when it could be a symptom: ringing ears, dizziness, chest pressure, sleep loss, heart racing, numbness. If a draft touches a physical symptom, the line points the viewer to a doctor, and the sign moves to perception or emotion.
- No mental health substitution. Nothing tells a viewer to read distress, dissociation or panic as activation, and nothing discourages professional care.
- No fear. Timing language stays a reading. No deadlines, no "before it is too late", no events presented as coming on a date.
- No conspiracy or political content. "Great Awakening" is also the name of a political conspiracy movement, so ACX scripts do not use that phrase on screen or in titles until Tee rules on it, and never touch elections, governments, vaccines or named public figures.
- Never invent a frequency, a code, a timeline, a protocol or an outcome.

## Short format (Short or Reel, 45 to 75 seconds)

1. Hook, 0 to 3 seconds: an identity or a sign that selects the viewer
2. Sign, 3 to 20 seconds: one specific perceptual, emotional or synchronicity marker
3. Map, 20 to 45 seconds: the consciousness concept the channel reads into the marker, said as a reading
4. Boundary, 45 to 60 seconds: what the concept does not guarantee and does not explain
5. Practice, 60 to 75 seconds: one observation or action the viewer can take today

Write the on-screen text lines beside the spoken lines, each naming the sign
or the concept for that beat.

## Long-form format

- Open with the same identity hook
- Stay with one concept for the full piece
- Show the sign in detail
- Name the interpretive step out loud, as interpretation
- End with the single practice

ACX has no long-form canon in the pipeline yet, so length and section count
are Tee's to set. Until he does, default to the studio long-form range of
1,200 to 2,000 TTS-ready words.

## Hook templates

These show shape. Each keeps the reading marked as a reading.

- "If the old timeline has felt thinner this week, here is how I read that."
- "Some of you have noticed the same small sign three times now. Here is what I think it points to."
- "This is not a physics lesson. It is the observer idea used as a lens on your own attention."

## Process

1. Ask Tee for the single sign, concept or experience to surface.
2. Require a concrete anchor: a perception, a repeated event or an observable pattern. Do not invent one.
3. Draft the Short first.
4. Strip any claim that cannot be checked, that promises a result, or that borrows physics as proof.
5. Add the on-screen text that names the sign or the concept.
6. Offer long-form only if the Short holds one idea cleanly.

## Constraints

- Never invent a frequency, a code, a timeline, a medical outcome or a source.
- Never claim a piece will go viral, and never promise a view count.
- Keep interpretive claims labelled as interpretive, and keep observation and belief apart.
- Stay inside the ACX constraints above and the hard rules in the user preferences.
- Platform policy, AI disclosure, medical misinformation and channel identity are compliance items with no exception path. If a draft touches one, flag it for Tee rather than deciding.
