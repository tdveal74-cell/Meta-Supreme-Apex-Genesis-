## The graph

`scripts/ltx_build_graph.py` writes the ComfyUI API prompt. Its wiring follows the shape of the official Comfy-Org `video_ltx2_3_i2v` template, from memory of that template and checked node by node against the pod's own node definitions (`GET /object_info`, saved as `object_info.json` in the scratchpad) on 2026-10-31; the template file itself was not opened on the pod: stage 1 at half size with the eight step distilled schedule `1.0, 0.99375, 0.9875, 0.98125, 0.975, 0.909375, 0.725, 0.421875, 0.0`, then `LTXVLatentUpsampler` x2 and a three step refine on `0.85, 0.7250, 0.4219, 0.0`; CFG 1.0, euler, `LTXVPreprocess` at compression 18 on every still; the first still through `LTXVImgToVideoInplace` at strength 0.7 in stage 1 and 1.0 in stage 2; the last still through `LTXVAddGuide` at `frame_idx` frames minus one, strength 1.0, at both stages, with `LTXVCropGuides` after each sampler; the audio latent path kept and discarded; `VAEDecodeTiled` 768, 64, 4096, 4; `CreateVideo` and `SaveVideo` under `video/`, and `SaveImage` under `frames/` when frames are wanted.

Two constraints shape every input: width and height divisible by 32, and frames equal to 8k plus 1. A 1080 still is padded to 1920x1088 with four replicated edge rows above and below and the output is cropped back to 1080 at conform; 241 frames is 10.04 s at 24 fps, 193 is 8.04 s, 225 is 9.375 s.

For a chained segment, `--first-frames` takes the previous segment's last nine frames (8k plus 1, model output, no preprocess), batches them with `ImageBatch` and adds them with `LTXVAddGuide` at `frame_idx` 0 at both stages, which the node's own source (`comfy_extras/nodes_lt.py` at ComfyUI v0.30.0, read 2026-10-31) describes as the path for a video guide. The seam is the ninth guided frame.

The pilot graph was built by an earlier version with a `ResizeImageMaskNode` per still; the two resize nodes were removed and the loaders wired straight into `LTXVPreprocess` before submission, 37 nodes as submitted, because the inputs were already 1920x1088. The builder now has no resize node.

`scripts/drift_scan.py` reads every fourth frame of a clip and prints the shift of the three built-in static regions against frame 0 and against the previous sample, by windowed phase correlation alone. That is the seed `align_still.py` starts from; `align_still` then refines it with an ECC translation fit, so the two read the same frame up to about 0.17 px apart (SH100 frame 96: 0.59 px by `align_still` on the lossless PNG against 0.42 by the scan on the mp4). The difference is the estimator, not the h264 decode, and a verifier reproduced it on 2026-10-31. `align_still` on the lossless frames is the measurement; the scan is the overview.
