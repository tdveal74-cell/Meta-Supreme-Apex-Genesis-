Note on how episodes are made

An AI model drafts each script and produces its narration.

When an avatar presents, it is built from footage of the man who makes this channel. None of it is a rented or stock persona.

Before an episode is published, an automated check reads its script. It holds any episode that states an outside statistic without a named source, including the "experts say" and "studies show" kind.

Nothing is published until a person has reviewed it and ticked it off by hand.
