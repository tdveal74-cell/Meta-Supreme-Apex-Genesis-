What happens to your email address

Note

Signup is not open yet. Until it opens, the form on the home page sends nothing and stores nothing.

What is kept

Your email address, and your first name if you give it. The form asks for nothing else.

When you send the form, the service that receives it also sees your IP address and browser details, which come with every web request.

The email provider may keep records of its own, such as the date you joined. Its own privacy policy covers those.

Why

To send you an email each time a new episode is published. If you give a first name, it is used to address those emails.

Where it is stored

With Hostinger Reach, the email service the list runs on.

How to leave

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Use it at any time and the emails stop. Your address stays on the list, marked unsubscribed.

Deletion

Unsubscribing does not delete your address or your name. There is no route yet for asking to have them deleted.
