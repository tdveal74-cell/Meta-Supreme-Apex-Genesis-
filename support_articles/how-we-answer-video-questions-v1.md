A useful answer should tell you what we know and where it came from.

For AI-assisted Support replies, the rule is simple: use an article I have approved, disclose the AI assistance, and get my approval before posting.

**How to ask a clear question**

Leave your question under the video it concerns. If you are asking about a particular statement, include the timestamp and the words you want clarified. If you tried a step from the video, explain what you expected and what happened.

Keep passwords, API keys and payment details out of public comments.

**Where the answer comes from**

The Support knowledge base holds articles I review and approve. An AI-assisted Support reply must cite one approved article as its source. A draft article is not an approved source.

That source needs to support the answer being given. Naming an article does not make an unrelated claim true. A question that goes beyond the approved material needs further review.

**Who reviews the reply**

AI may help prepare the wording. I must approve the reply before it is posted, and the reply must include an AI disclosure.

Approving an article and approving a reply are separate decisions. An approved article gives Support a source to use. It does not give permission to publish every answer based on it.

**What happens when there is no supported answer**

If the approved material does not answer your question, the answer is not ready. We should say what remains unverified rather than fill the gap with a confident guess.

That may mean checking the original episode, reviewing a source or preparing another article for approval. This process does not promise an immediate reply or a response to every comment.

**How to point out a possible error**

Include the video title or link, the timestamp and the specific statement you believe is wrong. If you have a source that helps explain the issue, include that too.

A correction needs to be checked against the evidence. The standard is the same for the original answer and the correction: check before making the claim.
